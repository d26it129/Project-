About Keval

I started building web pages to understand how the sites I use every day are put together. Since then I’ve learned that most of the work is small decisions: a clear heading, a button that’s big enough to tap, a layout that doesn’t break on a phone.

I’m currently learning front-end development and I’m happy to explain how each project here was made. Next, I want to get better at accessibility and at writing JavaScript that stays easy to read.
